📖 How to Use

  1. Put a small bright green/yellow marker on the pen tip.
  2. Press Start Camera.
  3. Make sure the colored tip is visible.
  4. Press Start Calibration.
  5. Move the pen tip to TOP-LEFT and press the calibration button.
  6. Move to TOP-RIGHT and press again.
  7. Move to BOTTOM-RIGHT and press again.
  8. Move to BOTTOM-LEFT and press again.
  9. Calibration will finish automatically.
  10. Move the pen in the air to write.
  11. Move the pen to the yellow PEN-UP area to stop the current stroke.
  12. Use Undo, Clear and Save PNG when required.
💡 Best setup: Use a small bright green/yellow tape or marker on the pen tip and use good lighting.
