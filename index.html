📖 How to Use

  1. Put a small bright green/yellow marker on the pen tip.
  2. Press Start Camera.
  3. Make sure the colored pen tip is visible.
  4. Press Calibrate.
  5. Move pen to TOP-LEFT and press Calibrate.
  6. Move pen to TOP-RIGHT and press Calibrate.
  7. Move pen to BOTTOM-RIGHT and press Calibrate.
  8. Move pen to BOTTOM-LEFT and press Calibrate.
  9. Calibration finishes automatically.
  10. Move the pen to write.
  11. Move pen to the yellow PEN-UP area to stop writing.
  12. Use Undo, Clear and Save when required.
💡 Best results: use a bright green/yellow marker on the pen tip and good lighting.
